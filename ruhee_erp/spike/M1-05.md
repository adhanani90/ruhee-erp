Nine decimals is **not a hard framework ceiling**: real columns and saved invoices
work at 12 on this installed bench. The standard DocField/Custom Field editor
still offers only 0-9. An app-owned Property Setter is accepted, but **fixtures/
Property Setters alone were insufficient in the observed migrate** for three
rate columns. A production candidate needs exported app metadata plus a verified
ordered schema-sync patch/after-migrate step, column assertions and a fresh source
rate/cache refresh. This spike is not a deployment recipe or a production fixture.

`cross_currency.synchronize_precision_columns` uses Frappe
`frappe/database/mariadb/database.py:MariaDBDatabase.updatedb` with freshly fetched
final metadata; `get_column_type` verifies the database. Precision is applied by
`frappe/custom/doctype/property_setter/property_setter.py:make_property_setter`
and `frappe/model/meta.py:Meta.apply_property_setters/get_field_precision`;
`frappe/database/schema.py:get_definition` permits the scale. No raw DDL or upstream
file edits. More scale reduces integer capacity at fixed width 21; finite
reciprocals and binary floats still require bill-size bounds.

A USD invoice's own conversion_rate is **1**. Widening that field alone cannot
repair its CDF payment valuation or add the 2320 settlement / 2300 base split.
PE target rate must also be accurate. Widening columns cannot recover old digits.

## Prototype mechanism and boundaries

`store_payment_prototype.store_rates` is an explicit process-scoped hook simulation
for the referenced store record, patching POS Awesome's invoice/tender resolvers
only within a context. `payment` calls ERPNext's `get_payment_entry` and ordinary
insert/submit: USD Debtors allocation, CDF cash at base and a pre-submit deduction
to Exchange income. `PaymentEntry.set_exchange_gain_loss` updates that deduction's
amount during validation while retaining its R7 account. No submitted GL or
currency validator is bypassed/edited. No persistent hook is registered.

The custom `Ruhee Spike Store Rate` DocType and `ruhee_spike_rate` links exist only
on scratch, created by guarded spike code; no production DocType is committed.
Nine-place counterexamples deliberately supply a truncated rate **inside the widened
PE column**, isolating lost source digits. Do not call those native inline results.

The proof combines the hook/orchestration and PE routes. Frontend integration,
offline capture/display/replay, idempotency, crash recovery, returns and prints
remain unverified. No fork was needed for this server proof; supported browser
extensions versus minimal POS fork still need investigation. Q1/Q2/Q5/Q6 stay open.

- Invoice `currency=USD`, company USD, Debtors account USD, invoice conversion_rate=1.
- Payment row `posa_payment_currency=CDF`, `posa_original_amount=232000` for C;
  standard `amount` is its invoice-currency USD equivalent, not the raw FC tender.
- `base_amount` is company USD; `posa_account_amount` is actual cash-account
  currency. Cash CDF's account currency must be CDF and its row's account must
  resolve to that account through the company-specific Mode of Payment mapping.
- Profile `posa_enable_multi_currency_payments=1` is the server feature gate;
  `posa_allow_multi_currency=1` exposes its UI. A `posa_allowed_currencies` row for
  CDF with `allow_for_payments=1` is needed for USD/company-USD invoices. Otherwise
  the server fallback allows only invoice/company currencies (both USD).
- For the browser, enable `posa_allow_payment_currency_selection` and choose CDF
  (or configure its default on the profile/payment method). Manual rate override
  additionally needs `posa_allow_manual_payment_exchange_rate`; it is not assumed
  for native tests. Same-currency CDF bills do not prove this cross-currency gate.
- Enabled Cash CDF Mode of Payment, a company account mapping, enabled CDF, a
  valid stored CDF->USD Currency Exchange rate, authorized profile membership,
  submitted/open shift for the current user, and a verified terminal cashier PIN.
- Use Sales Invoice mode (`create_pos_invoice_instead_of_sales_invoice=0`) and
  stock update for these tests. The separate POS Awesome Payments screen also
  requires `posa_use_pos_awesome_payments=1`; that is a gate in
  `api/payment_processing/processor.py:process_pos_payment`, not the inline
  Sales Invoice mixin's gate. No unspecified global POS Settings switch is assumed.

`posawesome/posawesome/api/payment_currency.py:normalize_invoice_payment_currencies`
resolves tender->invoice and invoice->company rates, derives company_rate as their
product, and resolves tender->cash-account currency separately. On a USD invoice
the company and invoice are the same, so native normalization gives one valuation
for tender settlement and cash. `set_multi_currency_change_amounts` treats paid
amount above invoice total as change. The mixin credits Debtors by payment base
amount and debits cash by that amount (subject to its change handling); it does
not append an Exchange income entry. A single Currency Exchange conversion does
not express both 2320 settlement and 2300 valuation. This source limitation is now supplemented by the measured account-currency submission failure above.

Schema prerequisites are defined by the installed
`posawesome/patches/add_per_payment_multi_currency_fields.py:execute`.

what was missing. `cross_currency.inspect` reports missing schema fields, profile
flags, allowed currencies, MOP account mappings/currencies, relevant Accounts/POS
Settings, enabled currencies and actual stored rates. Reports are limited to the

## Separate effects and large-case arithmetic

The line-price drift divides USD price by the truncated reciprocal. The Debtors
drift multiplies exact CDF bill amount by it; the exact 23,000,000 FC case bypasses
the frontend formula with a 230,000 FC unit price. For USD/CDF payments, the
invoice conversion_rate itself is **1**, so changing that field alone cannot
repair a truncated CDF payment rate or add an Exchange income allocation.

Expected C settlement GL: Dr Cash CDF 232,000 FC valued **$100.87**, Cr Debtors
**$100.00**, Cr Exchange income **$0.87** (in addition to the sale/stock GL).
For $10,000 paid with 23,200,000 FC at base 2300:

- Exact division: 23,200,000 / 2300 = 10,086.956521... -> **$10,086.96**, spread
  **$86.96** after allocating exactly $10,000 to Debtors.
- Nine-place reciprocal: 23,200,000 x 0.000434783 = 10,086.9656 -> **$10,086.97**,
  spread **$86.97** if the route sends the residual to Exchange income.

The Payment Entry counterexample now verifies this cent reaches Exchange income.
Native inline USD/CDF posting fails before committed GL, so the adapter's result
must not be substituted for a native pass. I2 and I4 routing alone do not catch the
incorrect R7 amount.

## Frontend rate precision and payment rounding: source findings

- `frontend/src/posapp/composables/pos/invoice/useInvoiceCurrency.ts:useInvoiceCurrency.update_currency_and_rate`
  assigns `conversion_rate.value = companyResult.rate || 0` in its exchange-rate
  resolver path, without rounding that rate to currency precision. Its local `flt`
  helper rounds amounts, and `useItemCurrency.ts:useItemCurrency` rounds item rates.
- `frontend/src/posapp/components/pos/invoice_utils/document.ts:get_invoice_doc`
  copies `context.conversion_rate` directly into the payload; `get_payments` copies
  `posa_exchange_rate` and `posa_company_exchange_rate`. No demonstrated frontend
  nine-place rate clamp in those paths. Browser verification is pending.
- `frontend/src/posapp/composables/pos/payments/usePaymentCurrencies.ts:usePaymentCurrencies`
  defines `normalizePayment`, `preciseTenderAmount` and `setInvoiceEquivalent`:
  rate values are assigned directly; `preciseTenderAmount` uses `toFixed(9)` on
  the **tender amount**, not the exchange rate. Converting from invoice equivalent
  divides by the resolved rate; `flt` rounds equivalent money to display precision.
- Neither that payment-normalization path nor backend
  `api/payment_currency.py:normalize_invoice_payment_currencies` invokes the native
  smallest-currency-fraction rounding helper. Source therefore does not establish
  R3/R5 tender rounding to 50 FC. The $1/2320 FC scratch case must capture what is
  saved; no nearest/up policy is implemented while Q1 is open.
- Changing precision is a candidate app-owned Property Setter/fixture, not a core
  edit. Nine is the editor's offered maximum; lower-level schema/resolver accepting
  12 was not proof of actual migration. This card must verify persisted decimal
  columns/new/old invoices on scratch before recommending it.
